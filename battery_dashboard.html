Battery health

A battery wears out slowly: over months the estimated runtime drops. One charge cycle = 100% charged in total (for example 2 × 50%).

Voltage after each full charge

Measured unplugged, 10+ minutes after reaching 100%, so the reading has settled. A slow drop over months is the earliest sign of battery wear; a few mV up or down between charges is normal.

Sessions

One row runs from one charge to the next, even across days and restarts. Sessions counts each time the headset was on for 10+ minutes (a PC restart or turning the headset off starts a new one). Headset on counts only that time, and the rate uses it.
